Listed by: Anonymous
Fiesta Square, Riverside Markets

Overview

Wander the plaza between food stalls serving fresh ceviche, elote, and slow-cooked birria while a live mariachi trio plays through the square. Try your hand at piñata breaking or the chilli tasting challenge, then cool off at the tequila and mezcal sampling bar as the afternoon heats up. Once the sun sets, the plaza turns into an open-air dance floor with a folklórico performance followed by a DJ set. Come hungry, come ready to dance, and stay for the fireworks that close out the night.

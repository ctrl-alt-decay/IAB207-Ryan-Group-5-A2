Bringing people together around the table

Epicurean is where food lovers find, book and host food events across Australia.

Our mission

Good food is better shared. From cooking classes and market tours to tastings and long-table dinners, Epicurean brings local food events into one place, so it's easy to find something worth going to and easy to put on something of your own.
